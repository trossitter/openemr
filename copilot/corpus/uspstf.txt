# USPSTF Recommendation Summary Table
# Source: U.S. Preventive Services Task Force
# https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation-topics
# Relevant demo patients: all five (broad intake flag coverage)

## CARDIOVASCULAR DISEASE

### Statin Use for the Primary Prevention of Cardiovascular Events in Adults
Grade: B
Population: Adults aged 40–75 years who have 1 or more CVD risk factors (dyslipidemia, diabetes, hypertension, or smoking) and an estimated 10-year CVD event risk of 10% or greater.
Recommendation: Prescribe a low- to moderate-dose statin for the primary prevention of CVD events and mortality.

### Blood Pressure in Adults: Screening
Grade: A
Population: Adults 18 years or older.
Recommendation: Screen for hypertension in adults 18 years or older. Obtain measurements outside of the clinical setting for diagnostic confirmation before starting treatment.

### Aspirin Use to Prevent Cardiovascular Disease
Grade: C (ages 40–59 with ≥10% 10-year CVD risk), D (age 60 or older)
Population: Adults aged 40–59 years with 10% or greater 10-year CVD risk.
Recommendation: Decision to initiate aspirin is an individual one. Evidence indicates that net benefit is small for this group. Recommend against initiating aspirin use in adults 60 years or older.

### Healthy Diet and Physical Activity for CVD Prevention in Adults With Cardiovascular Risk Factors
Grade: B
Population: Adults 18 years or older with cardiovascular disease risk factors.
Recommendation: Offer or refer adults with cardiovascular disease risk factors to intensive behavioral counseling interventions to promote healthful diet and physical activity.

## DIABETES

### Prediabetes and Type 2 Diabetes: Screening
Grade: B
Population: Adults aged 35–70 years who have overweight or obesity.
Recommendation: Screen for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes. Offer or refer to a prevention program for those with prediabetes.

### Gestational Diabetes Mellitus: Screening
Grade: B
Population: Asymptomatic pregnant persons.
Recommendation: Screen for gestational diabetes in asymptomatic pregnant persons at 24 weeks of gestation or after.

## CANCER SCREENING

### Colorectal Cancer: Screening
Grade: A (ages 45–75), B (ages 45–49)
Population: Adults aged 45–75 years.
Recommendation: Screen for colorectal cancer in all adults aged 45–75 years. Modalities include stool-based tests (FIT annually, high-sensitivity gFOBT annually, multi-target stool DNA every 1–3 years), direct visualization tests (colonoscopy every 10 years, CT colonography every 5 years, flexible sigmoidoscopy every 5 years), or flexible sigmoidoscopy every 10 years + FIT annually.

### Lung Cancer: Screening
Grade: B
Population: Adults aged 50–80 years who have a 20 pack-year smoking history and currently smoke or have quit within the past 15 years.
Recommendation: Annual screening for lung cancer with low-dose computed tomography (LDCT). Discontinue when person has not smoked for 15 years or develops a health problem substantially limiting life expectancy or willingness to have curative lung surgery.

### Breast Cancer: Screening
Grade: B
Population: Women aged 40 years or older.
Recommendation: Biennial screening mammography for women aged 40–74 years. Decision to start at age 40 should be an individual one.

### Cervical Cancer: Screening
Grade: A
Population: Women aged 21–65 years.
Recommendation: Screen for cervical cancer every 3 years with cervical cytology alone for women aged 21–29. For women aged 30–65, screen every 3 years with cervical cytology alone, every 5 years with hrHPV testing alone, or every 5 years with hrHPV testing in combination with cytology (co-testing).

### Prostate Cancer: Screening (PSA-Based)
Grade: C
Population: Men aged 55–69 years.
Recommendation: Decision to undergo periodic PSA-based screening is an individual one. Evidence that PSA-based screening prevents prostate cancer death is small; the potential harms are moderate. Men should have an opportunity to discuss potential benefits and harms with their clinician.

## MENTAL HEALTH

### Depression in Adults: Screening
Grade: B
Population: General adult population, including pregnant and postpartum persons.
Recommendation: Screen for depression in the general adult population. Adequate systems in place to ensure accurate diagnosis, effective treatment, and appropriate follow-up.

### Anxiety Disorders in Adults: Screening
Grade: B
Population: Adults younger than 65 years, including pregnant and postpartum persons.
Recommendation: Screen for anxiety disorders in adults younger than 65 years.

### Unhealthy Alcohol Use in Adolescents and Adults: Screening and Counseling
Grade: B
Population: Adults 18 years or older, including pregnant persons.
Recommendation: Screen for unhealthy alcohol use in primary care settings in adults 18 years or older and provide brief behavioral counseling interventions to reduce unhealthy alcohol use.

## INFECTIOUS DISEASE

### Hepatitis C Virus Infection in Adolescents and Adults: Screening
Grade: B
Population: Adults aged 18–79 years.
Recommendation: Screen for hepatitis C virus (HCV) infection in adults aged 18–79 years.

### HIV Infection: Screening
Grade: A
Population: Adolescents and adults aged 15–65 years. Younger adolescents and older adults who are at increased risk also benefit.
Recommendation: Screen for HIV infection in adolescents and adults aged 15–65 years.

### Sexually Transmitted Infections: Behavioral Counseling
Grade: B
Population: Sexually active adolescents and adults at increased risk for STIs.
Recommendation: Provide intensive behavioral counseling to all sexually active adolescents and to adults who are at increased risk for sexually transmitted infections.

## OBESITY AND WEIGHT

### Weight Loss to Prevent Obesity-Related Morbidity and Mortality in Adults: Behavioral Counseling
Grade: B
Population: Adults with a body mass index (BMI) of 30 or higher.
Recommendation: Offer or refer adults with a BMI of 30 or higher to intensive, multicomponent behavioral intervention.

### Healthy Weight and Weight Gain in Pregnancy: Behavioral Counseling
Grade: B
Population: Pregnant persons.
Recommendation: Provide intensive behavioral counseling interventions to promote healthful weight gain and prevent excess weight gain in pregnancy.

## RESPIRATORY

### Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD): Screening
Grade: D
Population: Asymptomatic adults.
Recommendation: Do not screen for COPD in asymptomatic adults using spirometry. (Note: symptomatic patients with risk factors should be evaluated clinically.)

## BONE HEALTH

### Osteoporosis to Prevent Fractures: Screening
Grade: B
Population: Women aged 65 years or older; postmenopausal women younger than 65 at increased risk.
Recommendation: Screen for osteoporosis with bone measurement testing to prevent osteoporotic fractures.
